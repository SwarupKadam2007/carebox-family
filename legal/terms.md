## 1. What CareBox Family Does

CareBox Family is a **medicine adherence companion app** for elderly adults ("Parent") and their family members ("Family"). It:

- Displays dose status (taken / not yet / late) for morning, afternoon, and night medicines.
- Lets a connected smart pillbox confirm doses automatically via a single button press.
- Allows Family members to send **one gentle, kind reminder** per missed dose per day.
- Tracks medicine inventory and suggests when refills may be needed.

CareBox Family does **not**:

- Dispense, prescribe, or recommend medicines.
- Replace a doctor, pharmacist, or carer.
- Access location, camera, microphone, or contacts.
- Store or transmit health data to any third party for advertising.

## 4. The Parent's Dignity and Control

- The **Parent** retains full control over sharing. They (or a Family admin acting on their behalf) may **pause sharing** at any time, immediately hiding all dose data from Family members.
- Pausing sharing does not delete any records — data is simply invisible to non-admin viewers until sharing is resumed.
- Family members will see "Sharing paused" and no dose details when the Parent has activated privacy mode.

---

## 5. Gentle Nudge — Rules and Limitations

- A family member may send a maximum of **one** gentle reminder per dose slot per day.
- Reminders are displayed as **in-app notifications and/or Web Push notifications** on the Parent's connected device, if the Parent has subscribed to push notifications.
- Reminders are worded as kind, non-alarming messages (e.g., "Mom hasn't confirmed her morning medicine yet. Just checking in 💙").
- The Service will **never** use loud alarms, red warnings, or language implying emergency unless directed by the user.

## 6. Medicine Data

- Medicine names, dosages, and schedules are stored securely in your family's private Supabase database.
- This data is protected by Row Level Security (RLS): only you and the family members you invite can access it.
- We do not sell, share, or use medicine data for advertising, research, or any commercial purpose.
- You can export all your data at any time from **Settings → Download My Data**.
- You can delete your account from **Settings → Delete Account**. Deletion anonymises all personal data within 48 hours.
